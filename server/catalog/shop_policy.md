Pickup and delivery
- Pickup points in Kyiv, Kharkiv, Odesa, Dnipro, Cherkasy. Pickup is free, moving the item from the warehouse to the pickup point is free.
- Shop courier delivery to the building entrance in Kyiv, Odesa, Kharkiv, Dnipro, Cherkasy. The price depends on the item size and is shown on the product page. Delivery takes from 3 days, depending on which warehouse the item is in.
- Other cities: shipped by Nova Poshta or Meest Express. Moving the item to the carrier is free; the customer pays the carrier's price, plus 2% money transfer fee and 20 UAH envelope for cash on delivery. Takes from 3 days. Some items need a wooden crate, which can double the shipping price; a manager confirms.
- Exact delivery dates for a city, and size limits of a particular carrier branch, are not on the site: a manager confirms.

Carrying upstairs (paid separately, per floor; an elevator counts as 2 floors)
- small appliances: free; microwaves, vacuums: 25 UAH; TVs, hobs, hoods, built-in microwaves: 50 UAH; ovens, water heaters: 75 UAH
- cookers, chest freezers, dishwashers, washers, washer-dryers, air conditioners, fridges up to 1 m tall: 100 UAH; fridges over 1 m tall: 150 UAH; side-by-side fridges: 200 UAH

Payment
- Pay on receipt, after unpacking and checking the item. Every item comes with a receipt and a filled warranty card.
- Cash on delivery for appliances over 1000 UAH, and large items sent by a carrier: 10% prepayment.
- Bank transfer: the price is confirmed by a manager. Visa and Mastercard accepted.
- The order price can be re-confirmed because of market conditions.

Installments and credit
- Pay in parts with monobank (3 to 25 payments) or PrivatBank (3, 6, 9 or 12 payments, from 300 UAH), first payment at purchase. Also PUMB, A-Bank, Sense Bank.
- Bank credit 300 to 150 000 UAH, 3 to 36 months, bank rate 0% to 3%; needs a Ukrainian passport and tax ID, age 21 to 70, a job.
- Whether a promo (red) price holds for a given number of payments is not on the site: a manager confirms.

Warranty and returns
- Two kinds of warranty: the official manufacturer warranty (repairs at an authorised service centre), or, when there is no manufacturer card, a shop warranty of 12 months on any appliance, repaired in the shop's own service centre. Which one a listing has is not in the catalog: a manager confirms.
- Return or exchange within 14 days under the consumer protection law, if unused, with packaging, seals, labels and the receipt.
